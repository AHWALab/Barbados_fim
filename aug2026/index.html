Likelihood that flood depth exceeds a threshold, computed by matching every ensemble member to a library of 200 pre-simulated flood scenarios for the parish and counting members pixel by pixel. Barbados is a pluvial site: members are matched by their rainfall total over the area of concern. There is no fluvial routine here, so the combined view of the Guatemala product does not apply.

Read this before interpreting.
1. Overbank only removes pixels that are already wet in the near zero inflow reference scenario. Saint Thomas has no permanent channel in that scenario, so the overbank and raw maps are identical here. The control is kept for consistency with the sites that do have a fluvial library.
2. Cycle 17 August 15:00 does not belong to this site. Its rasters are 308 by 271 cells on the Saint Michael window, which they cover in full, while every other cycle is 275 by 252 on Saint Thomas. Its trigger summary also reports 0.9, below the trigger of 1.0, and reads quiet. The cycle is kept in the viewer, drawn on its own window and badged, because finding it is part of the exercise. It is excluded from the Saint Thomas sequence and from the impact product.
