Barbados, probabilistic flood inundation maps

AHWA Laboratory, University of Iowa EWS-F project, funded by the WMO, 2026 Flood potential   Impact forecast

Each map is the likelihood that flood depth exceeds a threshold, computed by matching every member of the 50 member ensemble to a library of 200 pre-simulated flood scenarios for the parish and counting members pixel by pixel. A parish product is issued when the maximum unit streamflow of any member over the parish passes 1.0 m3/s per km2. Depth thresholds are 0.10, 0.30, 0.70 and 1.00 m. Barbados is a pluvial site, so members are matched on their rainfall total over the parish.

Every parish passed the trigger by a wide margin, so the viewer holds eleven parish products and an island mosaic clipped parish by parish. Member rain totals of 120 to 380 mm match 27 to 35 distinct library storms per parish. The two cases bracket the scale. In Tomas the trigger is passed everywhere and the question is where the water goes; in August 2026 the trigger is the story. Companion products for both cases: the UQ flood potential viewer shows the island wide hydrological guidance that decides whether a parish product runs at all, and the impact based forecast viewer turns these likelihood grids into warning levels for buildings, roads and districts.
