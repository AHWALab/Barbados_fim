Likelihood that flood depth exceeds a threshold, computed by matching every ensemble member to a library of 200 pre-simulated flood scenarios for the parish and counting members pixel by pixel. Barbados is a pluvial site: members are matched by their rainfall total over the parish. All eleven parishes passed the trigger in this cycle, so the island view is the mosaic of eleven parish products, each clipped to its own parish.

Likelihood at a pixel is the share of the 50 members whose matched scenario reaches the depth threshold there.

Read this before interpreting.
1. The trigger is passed everywhere. Maximum unit streamflow over the parishes runs from 18 to 28.6 m3/s per km2 against a trigger of 1.0, and in every parish all 50 members pass it. The question in this case is not whether a map is issued but where the water goes.
2. Member rain totals of 120 to 380 mm sit near the middle of the scenario libraries, so each member finds a distinct library storm. Between 27 and 35 different storms are matched per parish, which is where the likelihood spread comes from.
3. Overbank equals raw. Barbados has no permanent channel in the near zero inflow reference scenario, so the overbank variant removes nothing and is not offered here.
4. In the buffer strips between parishes the neighbouring products disagree, because each is matched on its own parish rainfall. The island view resolves this by clipping; the parish views show the full window, buffer included.
